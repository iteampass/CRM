# Legal pages

Public routes: `/terms-of-service`, `/privacy-policy`, `/cookie-policy`.
Content lives in `src/lib/legal-content.ts`, with Serbian translations in `src/lib/locales/sr.json`. All pages use the shared language selector and landing header/footer.

Operator supplied by the owner: Team Pass, Serbia. The owner requested a generic address; the pages therefore identify the country and contact email without inventing a street address.

Before treating the notices as final legal documents, the operator should confirm its registered identity and postal address, actual retention periods, provider processing regions and transfer arrangements, and any hosting-dashboard tracking enabled outside this repository. The cookie text describes the application code; no advertising or analytics scripts were added. This change does not introduce a consent mechanism or alter data processing.

Reference guidance used for privacy rights and cookies:
- https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/internet-telecoms/data-protection-online-privacy/
- https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/growing/digitalising/online-privacy/index_en.htm

Legal review should account for Serbian law and any other jurisdictions where the service operates. No exclusive jurisdiction clause or blanket exclusion of mandatory consumer rights is included.
